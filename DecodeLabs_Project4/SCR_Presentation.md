# Executive Briefing: Fulfillment Pipeline Analysis

## Situation
The current fulfillment pipeline is processing 1200 total requests per quarter. Successful throughput currently sits as 466 completed orders (Delivered and Shipped).

## Complication
Data extraction reveals a severe structural failure in the mid-funnel. 497 orders were explicitly classified as 'Cancelled' or 'Returned'. This represents a 41.4% leakage rate, causing immediate and significant revenue attrition before final realization.

## Resolution
1. **Immediate Hold:** Pause top-of-funnel marketing spend on historically highly-returned product categories.
2. **Supply Chain Audit:** Launch a mandatory 7-day technical audit into the logistics handoff process to identify the exact breakage point causing the massive cancellation volume.
